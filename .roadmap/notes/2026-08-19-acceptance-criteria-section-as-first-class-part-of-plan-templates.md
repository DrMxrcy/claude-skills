# Acceptance criteria section as first-class part of plan templates

From todowrite research: it separates requirement vs acceptance criteria vs constraint as distinct layers. Lightweight adoption for roadmap: add an 'Acceptance' section to plan templates (feature-plan.md, bug-investigation.md, refactor-debt.md) — explicit pass/fail conditions the spec-review and quality-review subagents check against. Template-only change; no config schema change. Optionally have review prompts in SKILL.md reference the Acceptance section explicitly.
